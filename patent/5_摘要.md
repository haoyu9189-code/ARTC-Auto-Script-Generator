# 摘要

## 发明名称

一种增材制造晶格结构力学性能数据库的快速构建方法及智能应用系统

---

## 摘要（中文，300字以内）

本发明公开了一种增材制造晶格结构力学性能数据库的快速构建方法及智能应用系统，属于增材制造与数据驱动设计领域。方法包括：定义包含24种晶胞拓扑类型的参数空间；自动遍历参数空间生成预处理脚本、求解器输入文件和后处理脚本；采用三阶段分离执行流程，第一阶段生成输入文件后退出释放资源，第二阶段并行求解，第三阶段提取数据，实现并行化计算和断点续算；对力-位移数据进行收敛检测、均匀插值和标准化输出。智能应用方法包括：基于归一化距离的单特征检索，返回最接近目标值的材料；基于响应曲面等值线交点的双特征多目标优化，找到同时满足两个性能目标的参数组合。系统还包括动态接触面检测模块，自动识别晶胞与刚性板的接触面，消除硬编码配置。本发明将数据库构建效率提升百倍，后处理成功率达98%，实现材料秒级检索和反向设计。

---

## Abstract（英文，300词以内）

The present invention discloses a method for rapid construction of mechanical property database of additive manufacturing lattice structures and an intelligent application system thereof, belonging to the field of additive manufacturing and data-driven design. The method comprises: defining a parameter space including 24 types of lattice cell topologies; automatically traversing the parameter space to generate preprocessing scripts, solver input files, and postprocessing scripts; adopting a three-phase separated execution process, wherein the first phase generates input files and exits to release resources, the second phase performs parallel solving, and the third phase extracts data, enabling parallel computation and checkpoint recovery; performing convergence detection, uniform interpolation, and standardized output on force-displacement data. The intelligent application method includes: single-feature retrieval based on normalized distance, returning materials closest to target values; dual-feature multi-objective optimization based on response surface contour intersection, finding parameter combinations satisfying two performance targets simultaneously. The system also includes a dynamic contact surface detection module that automatically identifies contact surfaces between lattice cells and rigid plates, eliminating hard-coded configurations. The invention improves database construction efficiency by a hundredfold, achieves 98% postprocessing success rate, and enables second-level material retrieval and inverse design.
